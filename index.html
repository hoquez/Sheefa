Duas for Cure

Supplications for healing from the Quran and the Sunnah, each with its Arabic text, meaning and reference.

Healing is from Allah alone. These duas go alongside medical care and do not replace it, so please keep seeing your doctor.

For the sick

Duas to say for those who are ill.

Lord of mankind, remove the suffering

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، أَذْهِبِ الْبَأْسَ، وَاشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لَا شِفَاءَ إِلَّا شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لَا يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا

Allaahumma Rabban-naas, adhhibil-ba's, washfi antash-Shaafi, laa shifaa'a illaa shifaa'uka, shifaa'an laa yughaadiru saqaman

O Allah, Lord of mankind, do away with my suffering. Heal (me), as You are the only Healer, and there is no cure except Yours, a cure that leaves no ailment behind.

The Prophet ﷺ would say this when visiting a sick person, wiping them with his right hand. When saying it for someone else, "heal him" or "heal her" is meant.

View the original dua card
Al-Bukhari 5675, Muslim 2191 (close wording). Reference: Authentic-Dua.com

The ruqyah of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ مُذْهِبَ الْبَاسِ اشْفِ أَنْتَ الشَّافِي لاَ شَافِيَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، شِفَاءً لاَ يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا

Allaahumma Rabban-naas, mudhhibal-ba's, ishfi antash-Shaafi, laa shaafiya illaa anta, shifaa'an laa yughaadiru saqaman

O Allah, Lord of mankind, Remover of suffering, heal, for You are the Healer. There is no healer but You, a healing that leaves no ailment behind.

Anas ibn Malik recited this for Thabit, who was unwell.

Al-Bukhari 5742

The cure is in Your hand

امْسَحِ الْبَاسَ رَبَّ النَّاسِ، بِيَدِكَ الشِّفَاءُ، لاَ كَاشِفَ لَهُ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ

Imsahil-ba's, Rabban-naas, biyadikash-shifaa', laa kaashifa lahu illaa anta

Remove the suffering, Lord of mankind. The cure is in Your hand, and no one can lift it except You.

Al-Bukhari 5744

When visiting the sick (seven times)

أَسْأَلُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ رَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ أَنْ يَشْفِيَكَ

As'alullaahal-'Azeem, Rabbal-'Arshil-'Azeem, an yashfiyak

I ask Allah the Magnificent, Lord of the Magnificent Throne, to cure you.

Say it seven times with a sick person whose appointed time has not come. The Prophet ﷺ said Allah will then cure them of that illness.

Abu Dawud 3106, At-Tirmidhi 2083. Hisnul Hasin p. 277

Comforting the sick

لاَ بَأْسَ طَهُورٌ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

Laa ba'sa, tahoorun in shaa' Allaah

No harm, it is a purification, if Allah wills.

The Prophet ﷺ would say this when he went in to visit a sick person.

Al-Bukhari 3616. Hisnul Hasin p. 275

In the name of Allah, the soil of our land

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تُرْبَةُ أَرْضِنَا بِرِيقَةِ بَعْضِنَا، يُشْفَى سَقِيمُنَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّنَا

Bismillaah, turbatu ardinaa, bireeqati ba'dinaa, yushfaa saqeemunaa bi-idhni Rabbinaa

In the name of Allah, the soil of our land, with the saliva of some of us, our sick one is cured by the permission of our Lord.

The Prophet ﷺ would say this to a sick person.

Al-Bukhari 5745, Muslim 2194. Hisnul Hasin p. 275

Wiping with the right hand: remove the suffering, heal him

اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّ النَّاسِ أَذْهِبِ الْبَاسَ، اشْفِهِ وَأَنْتَ الشَّافِي، لاَ شِفَاءَ إِلاَّ شِفَاؤُكَ، شِفَاءً لاَ يُغَادِرُ سَقَمًا

Allaahumma Rabban-naas, adhhibil-ba's, ishfihi wa antash-Shaafi, laa shifaa'a illaa shifaa'uka, shifaa'an laa yughaadiru saqaman

O Allah, Lord of mankind, remove the suffering. Heal him, for You are the Healer. There is no cure but Yours, a cure that leaves no ailment behind.

The Prophet ﷺ would pass his right hand over the sick person while saying this.

Al-Bukhari 5743, Muslim 2191 (close wording). Hisnul Hasin p. 275

O Allah, heal him

اللَّهُمَّ اشْفِهِ، اللَّهُمَّ عَافِهِ، اللَّهُمَّ اعْفِهِ

Allaahumma-shfihi, Allaahumma 'aafihi, Allaahumma a'fihi

O Allah, heal him. O Allah, grant him well-being. O Allah, spare him.

At-Tirmidhi 3564. Hisnul Hasin p. 277 (Al-Hakim, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Hibban, An-Nasa'i)

Calling the sick person by name

يَا فُلَانُ، شَفَى اللَّهُ سَقَمَكَ، وَغَفَرَ ذَنْبَكَ، وَعَافَاكَ فِي دِينِكَ وَجِسْمِكَ إِلَى مُدَّةِ أَجَلِكَ

Yaa fulaan, shafallaahu saqamaka, wa ghafara dhanbaka, wa 'aafaaka fee deenika wa jismika ilaa muddati ajalik

O (name of the person), may Allah cure your illness, forgive your sin, and keep you well in your religion and your body until the end of your term.

Al-Hakim, Al-Mustadrak 2014. Hisnul Hasin p. 277

O Forbearing, O Generous

يَا حَلِيمُ يَا كَرِيمُ اشْفِ فُلَانًا

Yaa Haleemu, yaa Kareemu, ishfi fulaanan

O Forbearing One, O Generous One, heal (name of the person).

A man told Ali that someone was ill. Ali asked if he would like that person to recover, then told him to say this.

Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah 31477 (Al-Shathri numbering). Hisnul Hasin p. 277

For your own pain

What to say when you are the one who is ill or in pain.

Place your hand where it hurts

بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ (٣)
أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ وَقُدْرَتِهِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا أَجِدُ وَأُحَاذِرُ (٧)

Bismillaah (three times). A'oodhu billaahi wa qudratihi min sharri maa ajidu wa uhaadhiru (seven times)

In the name of Allah. I seek refuge in Allah and His power from the evil of what I feel and what I fear.

Uthman ibn Abi al-As complained of pain in his body. The Prophet ﷺ told him to place his hand on the place that hurt and say this.

Muslim 2202

Reciting and blowing over yourself

During his final illness the Prophet ﷺ recited the Mu'awwidhat, blew on his hands and passed them over his face. The text of Surah Al-Falaq and Surah An-Nas is in the Quran section below.

Al-Bukhari 5735

The dua of Yunus, during illness (forty times)

لَّآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّآ أَنتَ سُبْحَٰنَكَ إِنِّى كُنتُ مِنَ ٱلظَّٰلِمِينَ

Laa ilaaha illaa anta subhaanaka innee kuntu minaz-zaalimeen

There is no god except You. Glory be to You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers.

Hisnul Hasin reports that whoever says this forty times in illness and dies of it receives the reward of a martyr, and if they recover, their sins are forgiven.

Quran 21:87. At-Tirmidhi 3505. Hisnul Hasin p. 278 (Al-Hakim)

To keep saying during illness

لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لاَ شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ وَلاَ حَوْلَ وَلاَ قُوَّةَ إِلاَّ بِاللَّهِ

Laa ilaaha illallaahu wallaahu akbar, laa ilaaha illallaahu wahdah, laa ilaaha illallaahu wahdahu laa shareeka lah, laa ilaaha illallaahu lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, laa ilaaha illallaahu wa laa hawla wa laa quwwata illaa billaah

There is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest. There is no god but Allah alone. There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. There is no god but Allah; His is the dominion and His is the praise. There is no god but Allah, and there is no might or power except with Allah.

The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says these words in illness and then dies, the Fire will not touch them.

At-Tirmidhi 3430, Ibn Majah 3794. Hisnul Hasin p. 278

Ruqyah

Words of healing and protection, including what the angel Jibril recited for the Prophet ﷺ.

The ruqyah of Jibril

بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ مِنْ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ يُؤْذِيكَ مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ نَفْسٍ أَوْ عَيْنِ حَاسِدٍ اللَّهُ يَشْفِيكَ بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ

Bismillaahi arqeek, min kulli shay'in yu'dheek, min sharri kulli nafsin aw 'ayni haasidin, Allaahu yashfeek, bismillaahi arqeek

In the name of Allah I recite over you, from everything that harms you, from the evil of every soul or envious eye. May Allah cure you. In the name of Allah I recite over you.

Muslim 2186. Hisnul Hasin pp. 275-276

From every disease

بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ يُبْرِيكَ وَمِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ يَشْفِيكَ وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ وَشَرِّ كُلِّ ذِي عَيْنٍ

Bismillaahi yubreek, wa min kulli daa'in yashfeek, wa min sharri haasidin idhaa hasad, wa sharri kulli dhee 'ayn

In the name of Allah, may He make you well and cure you of every disease, and from the evil of an envier when he envies, and the evil of every evil eye.

Jibril recited this when the Prophet ﷺ was unwell.

Muslim 2185

From every disease within you (three times)

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ وَاللَّهُ يَشْفِيكَ مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ فِيكَ مِنْ شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ وَمِنْ شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

Bismillaahi arqeeka, wallaahu yashfeeka min kulli daa'in feeka, min sharrin-naffaathaati fil-'uqadi, wa min sharri haasidin idhaa hasad

In the name of Allah I recite over you, and Allah will cure you of every disease within you, from the evil of those who blow on knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies.

Ibn Majah 3524. Hisnul Hasin p. 276 (Ibn Abi Shaybah, An-Nasa'i)

From the evil of every envier (three times)

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ أَرْقِيكَ مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ يَشْفِيكَ مِنْ شَرِّ كُلِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ وَمِنْ كُلِّ ذِي عَيْنٍ

Bismillaahi arqeeka min kulli daa'in yashfeeka, min sharri kulli haasidin idhaa hasad, wa min kulli dhee 'ayn

In the name of Allah I recite over you. May He cure you of every disease, from the evil of every envier when he envies, and from every evil eye.

Hisnul Hasin p. 276 (Ahmad)

Good health

Asking Allah for well-being, and for protection from illness.

Health in body, hearing and sight (morning and evening)

اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَدَنِي اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي سَمْعِي اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَصَرِي لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ

Allaahumma 'aafinee fee badanee, Allaahumma 'aafinee fee sam'ee, Allaahumma 'aafinee fee basaree, laa ilaaha illaa anta

O Allah, grant me health in my body. O Allah, grant me health in my hearing. O Allah, grant me health in my sight. There is no god but You.

Abu Bakrah repeated this three times each morning and evening, following the Prophet ﷺ.

Abu Dawud 5090

Well-being in this world and the next

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ

Allaahumma innee as'alukal-'aafiyata fid-dunyaa wal-aakhirah

O Allah, I ask You for well-being in this world and the Hereafter.

The opening of a dua the Prophet ﷺ never left, morning or evening.

Abu Dawud 5074, Ibn Majah 3871

Refuge from serious diseases

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْبَرَصِ وَالْجُنُونِ وَالْجُذَامِ وَمِنْ سَيِّئِ الأَسْقَامِ

Allaahumma innee a'oodhu bika minal-barasi wal-junooni wal-judhaami wa min sayyi'il-asqaam

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from leprosy, madness, elephantiasis and all evil diseases.

Abu Dawud 1554

From the Quran

The dua of Prophet Ayyub and the surahs used for ruqyah.

The dua of Ayyub (peace be upon him)

أَنِّى مَسَّنِىَ ٱلضُّرُّ وَأَنتَ أَرْحَمُ ٱلرَّٰحِمِينَ

Annee massaniyad-durru wa anta arhamur-raahimeen

Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.

Allah says in the next verse: "So We responded to him and removed what afflicted him of adversity."

Quran 21:83-84

Ayyub's call in his hardship

أَنِّى مَسَّنِىَ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ بِنُصْبٍۢ وَعَذَابٍ

Annee massaniyash-shaytaanu binusbin wa 'adhaab

Indeed, Satan has touched me with hardship and torment.

Quran 38:41

Surah Al-Fatihah, the ruqyah

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (١) ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَٰلَمِينَ (٢) ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ (٣) مَٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ (٤) إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ (٥) ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ (٦) صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ (٧)

In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, Lord of the worlds, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful, Sovereign of the Day of Recompense. It is You we worship and You we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path, the path of those upon whom You have bestowed favor, not of those who have evoked Your anger or of those who are astray.

A Companion recited it over a tribal chief who had been stung, and he recovered. The Prophet ﷺ asked, "How did you know that it is a ruqyah?"

Quran 1:1-7. Al-Bukhari 5736

Surah Al-Falaq

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ (١) مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ (٢) وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ (٣) وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِى ٱلْعُقَدِ (٤) وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ (٥)

Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak from the evil of that which He created, and from the evil of darkness when it settles, and from the evil of the blowers in knots, and from the evil of an envier when he envies."

Quran 113. Recited for illness: Al-Bukhari 5735

Surah An-Nas

قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ (١) مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ (٢) إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ (٣) مِن شَرِّ ٱلْوَسْوَاسِ ٱلْخَنَّاسِ (٤) ٱلَّذِى يُوَسْوِسُ فِى صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ (٥) مِنَ ٱلْجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ (٦)

Say, "I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the Sovereign of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil of the retreating whisperer, who whispers evil into the breasts of mankind, from among the jinn and mankind."

Quran 114. Recited for illness: Al-Bukhari 5735
